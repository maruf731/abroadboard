Getting around campus

Things we wish someone had told us in our first week on campus in Auckland.

Open in Google Maps

  1. Read room codes as building-room

    A room like 260-115 means building 260, room 115. Find the building number first, then the room.

  2. Get an AT HOP card

    Auckland buses, trains and ferries use AT HOP. Apply for the tertiary concession to pay less per trip.

  3. Leave ten minutes between classes

    The campus sits on a hill and some buildings are a steep walk apart. Plan back-to-back classes with that in mind.

  4. Find a study spot early

    The General Library and Kate Edger Information Commons fill up fast in exam season. Pick a backup spot in week one.

  5. Use Albert Park as your meeting point

    It's right next to campus and easy to find, which makes it a good default when meeting someone from the board.

  6. Get your student ID card in week one

    You need it for exams, the library, printing and building access, and it unlocks many student discounts.

  7. Use free health and counselling services

    Most institutions have a student health centre and free counselling. Book early, especially around exams.

  8. Ask for student discounts everywhere

    Show your student ID at cinemas, gyms, phone shops and some cafes. Many places take 10% off or more.

  9. Keep important numbers in your phone

    Save 111 for emergencies, your campus security number and your institution's international student office.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about joining, posting and buying and selling on abroadboard.

Getting started

Who can join abroadboard?

Students currently studying at one of the 16 accepted Auckland universities and colleges. You need a valid student email from an accepted institution. The full list is on the Join page.

How do I sign up?

Open Join, enter your student email, choose your campus area, confirm that you study in Auckland, add a display name and agree to the privacy notice. Then enter your verification code. We never ask for your passport or other personal documents.

I already have a profile. How do I sign in?

On the Join page, choose Sign in and enter the student email you joined with and your password.

Is abroadboard free?

Yes. Posting meetups, study buddies, housing, help requests, items for sale and food tips is free. Listing an event costs $5 NZD, which helps keep the site running.

Posting

How do posts work?

Choose a category, add a title and your details, say who the post is for, and pick how people can contact you. Posts expire after 21 days to keep the board fresh and relevant.

What should I include in my post?

A clear title, what you're looking for, when and where it happens, and how people should contact you. Never share your home address or passport details.

What if there's no category for my post?

Choose Other, and tap Request a category on the board to suggest a new one. An admin reviews every request, and approved categories appear for everyone.

What does "For:" on a post mean?

It's optional and shows who the post is meant for, such as women only, light or heavy sleepers, or a note like "non-smoker" or "first-year students". It's handy when looking for a flatmate or a study group.

What photos can I add?

Up to two photos per post, in .jpg or .jpeg format and 5 MB or smaller each, that help explain your meetup, item or tip. Don't upload personal documents, sensitive information, or people's faces without their consent.

How long do posts stay up?

Posts stay up for 21 days and are then removed automatically. Events disappear about a week after they happen. You can repost any time if you still need help, and delete your own post whenever you like.

Buy and Sell

How does Buy and Sell work?

List furniture, electronics, books, kitchenware, clothing, bikes and more. Each listing shows the price, condition, description, photos and how to get in touch. It's a simple way to save money, reuse things and trade locally. Meet in a public place and check the item before you pay.

Contact and safety

How do I contact another student?

Each post shows the contact methods the author offers: email, mobile or social media. Sign in and tap Request contact details. When the author shares them, they appear on the post for you.

Who can see my contact details?

Only the students you approve. Your email, mobile number or social media handle is never shown publicly, and never to people who aren't signed in.

What if I see a problem with a post?

Tap Report on the post. The admin team will review it and take action if needed. To tell us more, or to report a person, use the Contact page.

Clubs and businesses

Can student clubs post events?

Yes. Clubs and students can list events such as workshops, cultural nights and sports tournaments on the Events page for $5 NZD per listing.

Can businesses advertise on abroadboard?

Yes. Local businesses can book sponsor slots to reach Auckland's international student community. Email info@abroadboard.com to ask about it.

Your account

Can I use abroadboard in another language?

Yes. Use the language menu at the top of any page to switch between English, Chinese (中文), te reo Māori and French (Français).

Can I use dark mode?

Yes. abroadboard follows your device's light or dark setting. Tap the sun or moon button at the top of the page to switch.

How do interests and notifications work?

Pick the categories you care about when you sign up, or any time on My account. When someone posts in one of them, the bell at the top shows a notification with a link to the post. Tap Only your interests on the board to see just those posts.

How do I change or delete my account?

Change your display name and home country on My account. To delete your account or your data, email info@abroadboard.com.

How do I contact the abroadboard team?

Sign in and use the Contact page to send a question, complaint or report. An admin replies there and you get a notification. You can also email info@abroadboard.com.
