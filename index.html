Getting around campus

Five things we wish someone had told us in our first week at the City Campus.

Campus map goes here

Add the official University of Auckland City Campus map with key buildings marked.

  1. Read room codes as building-room

    A room like 260-115 means building 260, room 115. Find the building number first, then the room.

  2. Get an AT HOP card

    Auckland buses, trains and ferries use AT HOP. Apply for the tertiary concession to pay less per trip.

  3. Leave ten minutes between classes

    The campus sits on a hill and some buildings are a steep walk apart. Plan back-to-back classes with that in mind.

  4. Find a study spot early

    The General Library and Kate Edger Information Commons fill up fast in exam season. Pick a backup spot in week one.

  5. Use Albert Park as your meeting point

    It's right next to campus and easy to find, which makes it a good default when meeting someone from the board.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about joining, posting and buying and selling on abroadboard.

Who can join abroadboard?

Students at one of the 16 accepted Auckland universities and colleges. Sign up with your student email and confirm that you study in Auckland. The full list of accepted emails is on the Join page.

How do I sign up?

Open Join, enter your student email, pick the area of Auckland you study in, tick the two boxes and press the button. Then type the verification code to create your profile.

Is it free?

Yes. Posting on the board, selling items and sharing tips are free. Listing an event costs $5 NZD, which helps keep the site running.

How does Buy and Sell work?

Tap + Sell something, add a price, the condition and up to two photos. Buyers tap Request contact details, and you decide who gets them. Meet in a public place on or near campus, and check the item before you pay.

Who can see my contact details?

Nobody, until you tap Share on their request. Your email, mobile or social media handle is never shown publicly on a post.

What does "For:" on a post mean?

It shows who the post is meant for, such as women only, light sleepers, or non-smokers. Authors choose this when they post, for example when looking for a flatmate.

What photos can I add?

Up to two photos per post, in .jpg or .jpeg format, each 5 MB or smaller. Large photos are resized automatically so they load quickly.

How long do posts stay up?

Posts and their photos are removed automatically after 21 days. Events disappear about a week after they happen. You can delete your own post at any time.

I saw a scam or something inappropriate. What should I do?

Tap Report on the post and an admin will check it. For anything urgent, email info@abroadboard.com.

Can I use abroadboard in Chinese?

Yes. Tap 中文 at the top of any page to switch to Simplified Chinese, and English to switch back.

How do I change or delete my account?

Change your display name and home country on My account. To delete your account or your data, email info@abroadboard.com.
